## Opusculus Lamae Bal ta Mezzamortie / 《拉美‧巴爾與無歇之死》小論

**FormID**: 0x01AF40　**EditorID**: Book3ValuableOpusculusLamae

### 英文原文

Opusculus Lamae Bal ta Mezzamortie
A brief account of Lamae Bal and the Restless Death

Mabei Aywenil, Scribe
Translation by University of Gwylim Press; 3E 105

As brighter grows light, darker becomes shadow. So it passed that the Daedra Molag Bal looked on Arkay and thought the Aedra prideful of his dominion o'er the death of man and mer, and it was sooth.

Bal, whose sphere is the wanton oppression and entrapment of mortal souls, sought to thwart Arkay, who knew that not man, nor mer, nor beastfolk of all Nirn could escape eventual death. The Aedra was doubtless of his sphere, and so Molag Bal set upon Nirn to best death.

Tamriel was still young, and filled with danger and wondrous magick when Bal walked in the aspect of a man and took a virgin, Lamae Beolfag, from the Nedic Peoples. Savage and loveless, Bal profaned her body, and her screams became the Shrieking Winds, which still haunt certain winding fjords of Skyrim. Shedding a lone droplet of blood on her brow, Bal left Nirn, having sown his wrath.

Violated and comatose, Lamae was found by nomads, and cared for. A fortnight hence, the nomad wyrd-woman enshrouded Lamae in pall for she had passed into death. In their way, the nomads built a bonfire to immolate the husk. That night, Lamae rose from her funeral pyre, and set upon the coven, still aflame. She ripped the throats of the women, ate the eyes of the children, and raped their men as cruelly as Bal had ravished her.

And so; Lamae, (who is known to us as blood-matron) imprecated her foul aspect upon the folk of Tamriel, and begat a brood of countless abominations, from which came the vampires, most cunning of the night-horrors. And so was the scourge of undeath wrought upon Tamriel, cruelly mocking Arkay's rhythm of life and death through all the coming eras of the et'Ada, and for all his sadness, Arkay knew this could not be undone.

### 繁體中文翻譯

《拉美‧巴爾與無歇之死》小論

Mabei Aywenil，書記
格威林大學出版社譯；第三紀元105年

光愈明，影愈深。如是，黛德拉莫拉格‧巴爾凝視阿卡依，以為此艾德拉神靈自恃其對人族與梅爾族死亡之權柄，而此論斷不差。

巴爾之領域，乃凡俗靈魂之橫暴壓迫與囚禁。他欲挫阿卡依——阿卡依深知，無論人、梅爾，抑或大地寧所有獸族，終難逃一死。阿卡依對己之職守毫無疑惑，因此莫拉格‧巴爾降臨大地寧，誓要勝越死亡本身。

泰姆瑞爾尚年輕，危機與瑰奇魔法充盈其間，那時巴爾以人形行走於世，從內德人族中奪去一名處女，拉美‧貝奧爾法格。巴爾凌辱她的肉身，殘暴而絕無柔情，她的哭喊化作「嘯鳴風」，至今仍縈繞天際林某些蜿蜒峽灣。巴爾在她額頭滴下孤零一滴血，便離棄大地寧，播下了他的憤怒之種。

拉美遭受凌辱，昏迷不醒，被游牧之人尋獲，得到照料。半月之後，部落的女巫判定她已死去，以裹屍布包裹其形骸。依照他們的習俗，游牧人建起篝火，欲燒化這具軀殼。然而那夜，拉美從葬火中起身，仍然燃燒著火焰，向部落人群撲去。她撕裂婦人的咽喉，啖食孩童的眼睛，以她當初被巴爾凌辱時同等的殘忍，暴虐那些男人。

如此這般，拉美——她被我們稱為血之聖母——將她污穢的本質詛咒在泰姆瑞爾子民身上，誕下無數怪物的血脈，由此誕生了吸血鬼——夜中最為狡詐的幽靈。不死之禍由此降臨泰姆瑞爾，殘酷地嘲弄著阿卡依生死輪迴的秩序，貫穿et'Ada諸神全部的世代。即便充滿悲哀，阿卡依也知道，此事已無可挽回。

### 與 VIGILANT 的關聯

這是吸血鬼起源的正典文獻，直接道出莫拉格‧巴爾如何藉凌辱拉美‧貝奧爾法格創造了不死族，以此嘲弄阿卡依的生死秩序。VIGILANT 的核心衝突——史坦達爾鬥士對抗吸血鬼與莫拉格‧巴爾的勢力——正建立在這個神話事件之上。拉美此後化名「拉美‧巴爾」，成為第一代吸血鬼「血之聖母」，她被傷害後以同樣的傷害回饋世界，這種「受害者化身加害者的悲劇弧線」貫穿整個 VIGILANT 主線。

### 繁體中文翻譯

《聖艾蕾西婭的試煉》

阿卡托什在久遠前的那些歲月裡，與艾蕾西婭立下了盟約。他將混沌之境奧伯利文纏繞的絲縷聚攏，以其心臟的血腥筋脈緊緊編織，賜予艾蕾西婭，說道：「此乃我賜予汝之信物——只要汝之血脈與誓言堅守如初，我之血脈與誓言，亦將對汝堅守如初。此信物乃諸王之護符，吾等之盟約由此而立。我為靈魂之王，汝為凡人之后。汝為一切凡骨見證，我為一切永恆靈魂見證。」

只要龍之血脈在統治者身上強盛流淌，帝國的榮耀便將連綿不斷。然而若龍焰熄滅，又若無我們合血之後裔佩戴諸王之護符，帝國便將沉淪黑暗，失序的惡魔諸王將統治這片土地。」

——摘自《重燃龍火》禮典

### 與 VIGILANT 的關聯

阿卡托什-艾蕾西婭盟約是帝國神學的根基：龍焰阻隔奧伯利文之門，防止黛德拉湧入凡世。VIGILANT mod 中，莫拉格‧巴爾的「冷港」計畫正是要破壞此屏障。艾蕾西婭以自己的血脈與誓言換取了子民的自由——這與拉美‧巴爾被迫承受命運形成強烈對比：同樣是凡人女性遭遇神明或黛德拉，一者締立聖約，另一者被強加詛咒。

## Aedra and Daedra / 《艾德拉與黛德拉》

**FormID**: 0x01B22C　**EditorID**: Book2ReligiousAedraandDaedra

### 英文原文

Aedra and Daedra

The designations of Gods, Demons, Aedra, and Daedra, are universally confusing to the layman. They are often used interchangeably.

"Aedra" and "Daedra" are not relative terms. They are Elvish and exact. Azura is a Daedra both in Skyrim and Morrowind. "Aedra" is usually translated as "ancestor," which is as close as Cyrodilic can come to this Elven concept. "Daedra" means, roughly, "not our ancestors." This distinction was crucial to the Dunmer, whose fundamental split in ideology is represented in their mythical genealogy.

Aedra are associated with stasis. Daedra represent change.

Aedra created the mortal world and are bound to the Earth Bones. Daedra, who cannot create, have the power to change.

As part of the divine contract of creation, the Aedra can be killed. Witness Lorkhan and the moons.

The protean Daedra, for whom the rules do not apply, can only be banished.

### 繁體中文翻譯

《艾德拉與黛德拉》

「諸神」、「惡魔」、「艾德拉」、「黛德拉」這幾個稱謂，在俗人眼中向來令人困惑，往往被混用。

「艾德拉」與「黛德拉」並非相對性的詞語，而是精確的古精靈語。阿祖拉在天際省與晨風省都是黛德拉，從無歧義。「艾德拉」通常譯為「祖先」，這已是賽羅迪爾語所能表達的最接近詞語。「黛德拉」的意思大致是「非我等之祖先」。這一區分對於鄧梅爾人至關重要——他們意識形態上的根本分裂，正體現在神話譜系之中。

艾德拉與靜止相關聯；黛德拉代表變化。

艾德拉創造了凡俗世界，並受縛於大地之骨。黛德拉無法創造，卻擁有改變的力量。

作為創世神約的一部分，艾德拉是可以被殺死的——洛爾坎與月亮便是佐證。

而多變的黛德拉，不受規則約束，只能被放逐，無法被真正消滅。

### 與 VIGILANT 的關聯

這篇短論確立了整個 Elder Scrolls 宇宙觀的核心對立：艾德拉已付出代價創造世界並受其束縛，黛德拉則永遠遊離其外、只能被驅逐而不能被殺。VIGILANT 中史坦達爾鬥士面對黛德拉的根本困境——無論消滅多少，他們仍會回來——正是這一宇宙規則的體現。莫拉格‧巴爾無法被永久消滅，只能被驅逐，這解釋了為何對抗他的戰爭永無終結之日。

## The Song of Pelinal, v1 / 《培林納之歌》第一卷：論其名

**FormID**: 0x01AF8A　**EditorID**: Book2ReligiousSongofPelinalV1

### 英文原文

The Song of Pelinal, Volume 1: On His Name

[Editor's Note: Volumes 1-6 are taken from the so-called Reman Manuscript located in the Imperial Library. It is a transcription of older fragments collected by an unknown scholar of the early Second Era. Beyond this, little is known of the original sources of these fragments, some of which appear to be from the same period. But, as no scholarly consensus yet exists on dating these six fragments, no opinions will be offered here.]

That he took the name "Pelinal" was passing strange, no matter his later sobriquets, which were many. That was an Elvish name, and Pelinal was a scourge on that race, and not much given to irony. Pelinal was much too grim for that; even in youth he wore white hair, and trouble followed him. Perhaps his enemies named Pelinal of their own in their tongue, but that is doubtful, for it means "glorious knight", and he was neither to them. Certainly, many others added to that name during his days in Tamriel: he was Pelinal the Whitestrake because of his left hand, made of a killing light; he was Pelinal the Bloody, for he [drank] it in victory; he was Pelinal Insurgent, because he gave the crusades a face; he was Pelinal In Triumph, as the words eventually became synonymous, and men-at-arms gave thanks to the Eight when they saw his banner coming through war; he was Pelinal the Blamer, for he was quick to admonish those allies of his that favored tactics that ran counter to his, that is, sword-theory; and he was Pelinal the Third, though whether this was because some said he was a god guiser, who had incarnated twice before already, or that, simpler, he was the third vision given to Perrif, anon Alessia, in her prayers of liberation before he walked among the quarters of rebellion, is unknown.

### 繁體中文翻譯

《培林納之歌》第一卷：論其名

【編者按：第一至六卷摘自所謂的「雷曼抄本」，藏於帝國圖書館。此乃第二紀元初期某位不知名學者所收錄的更古老殘篇謄本。除此之外，這些殘篇的原始來源所知甚少，其中數篇似乎出自同一時期，甚或同一抄本。然由於學界對此六篇殘篇的年代尚無定論，本版不作任何推斷。】

他取名「培林納」，委實奇異——無論後來他得了多少稱號，此名本身便已怪異。那是一個精靈語名字，而培林納卻是那個種族的災星，且此人素來不會玩弄諷刺。培林納為人太過冷峻；即便年少時，他便生著一頭白髮，麻煩如影隨形。或許是敵人以他們的語言給他取了這個名字，但這似乎難以置信，因為此名的意思是「光榮的騎士」，在敵人眼中他既非光榮，亦非騎士。當然，在他縱橫泰姆瑞爾的歲月裡，許多人為他加諸了無數稱號：他是「白擊者培林納」，因為他的左手是由致命之光鑄成；他是「嗜血者培林納」，因為他在勝利中飲血；他是「反叛者培林納」，因為他給聖戰賦予了一張面孔；他是「凱旋者培林納」，因為這兩個詞最終成了同義詞，戰陣中的將士一見他的旗幟穿越戰場，便向八神致謝；他是「指責者培林納」，因為他對那些偏好與他劍術理論相左策略的盟友疾言厲色；他又是「第三培林納」——至於這是因為有人說他是偽裝成神的存在、此前已降世兩次，還是因為更簡單地，他是佩立芙（後來的艾蕾西婭）在解放祈禱中所見的第三道異象，早於他行走於反抗軍各部之間，至今成謎。

### 與 VIGILANT 的關聯

培林納的異名眾多、本質成謎（神化身？機械人？）呼應了 VIGILANT mod 對「英雄的本質是什麼」這個問題的追問。他一個精靈名、卻是精靈的災星，以及他可能是某種神聖機器的暗示，在 VIGILANT 中關於「神明介入凡俗事務的代價」這一主題中有所回響。

### 與 VIGILANT 的關聯

艾蕾西婭向神明的祈禱得到回應、「星辰鑄就的騎士」從神話時間降臨的敘事結構，與 VIGILANT 中玩家受到神明呼召、介入更大宇宙衝突的主題形成平行。培林納「從未來時代的鎧甲」這一細節，暗示了跨越時間的神聖干預——這是 VIGILANT mod 大量運用的主題。

## The Song of Pelinal, v3 / 《培林納之歌》第三卷：論其敵

**FormID**: 0x01AF8C　**EditorID**: Book2ReligiousSongofPelinalV3

### 英文原文

The Song of Pelinal, Volume 3: On His Enemy

[Editor's Note: as Volume 1.]

Pelinal Whitestrake was the enemy of all elfkind that lived in Cyrod in those days. Mainly, though, he took it upon himself to slay the sorcerer-kings of the Ayleids in pre-arranged open combats rather than at war; the fields of rebellion he left to the growing armies of the Paravania and his bull nephew. Pelinal called out Haromir of Copper and Tea into a duel at the Tor, and ate his neck-veins while screaming praise to Reman, a name that no one knew yet. ## On Oblivion / 《論奧伯利文》

**FormID**: 0x01ACF3　**EditorID**: Book3ValuableOnOblivion

### 英文原文

On Oblivion
by Morian Zenas

It is improper, however customary, to refer to the denizens of the dimension of Oblivion as "demons." This practice probably dates to the Alessian Doctrines of the First Era prophet Marukh -- which, rather amusingly, forbade "trafficke with daimons" and then neglected to explain what daimons were.

It is most probable that "daimon" is a misspelling or etymological rendition of "Daedra," the old Elven word for those strange, powerful creatures of uncertain motivation who hail from the dimension of Oblivion. ("Daedra" is actually the plural form; the singular is "Daedroth.") In a later tract by King Hale the Pious of Skyrim, almost a thousand years after the publication of the original Doctrines, the evil machinations of his political enemies are compared to "the wickedness of the demons of Oblivion... their depravity equals that of Sanguine itself, they are cruel as Boethiah, calculating as Molag Bal, and mad as Sheogorath." Hale the Pious thus long-windedly introduced four of the Daedra lords to written record.

But the written record is not, after all, the best way to research Oblivion and the Daedra who inhabit it. Those who "trafficke with daimons" seldom wish it to be a matter of public account. Nevertheless, scattered throughout the literature of the First Era are diaries, journals, notices for witch burnings, and guides for Daedra-slayers. These I have used as my primary source material. They are at least as trustworthy as the Daedra lords I have actually summoned and spoken with at length.

Apparently, Oblivion is a place composed of many lands -- thus the many names for which Oblivion is synonymous: Coldharbour, Quagmire, Moonshadow, etc. It may be correctly supposed that each land of Oblivion is ruled over by one prince. The Daedra princes whose names appear over and over in ancient records are the afore-mentioned Sanguine, Boethiah, Molag Bal, and Sheogorath, and in addition, Azura, Mephala, Clavicus Vile, Vaernima, Malacath, Hoermius (or Hermaeus or Hormaius or Herma -- there seems to be no one accepted spelling) Mora, Namira, Jyggalag, Nocturnal, Mehrunes Dagon, and Peryite.

From my experience, Daedra are a very mixed lot. It is almost impossible to categorize them as a whole except for their immense power and penchant for extremism. Mehrunes Dagon, Molag Bal, Peryite, Boethiah, and Vaernima are among the most consistently "demonic" of the Daedra, in the sense that their spheres seem to be destructive in nature. Molag Bal elects the employment of other daedra, and Boethiah inspires the arms of mortal warriors.

### 繁體中文翻譯

《論奧伯利文》
作者：莫里安‧澤納斯

將奧伯利文維度的居民稱為「惡魔」雖然習以為常，卻是不妥當的。這個習慣大概可追溯至第一紀元先知馬魯克的艾蕾西婭教義——頗為可笑的是，那部教義禁止「與鬼魔來往」，卻未曾解釋鬼魔究竟是什麼。

「鬼魔」極可能是「黛德拉」的誤寫或音譯，後者是古精靈語中對那些來自奧伯利文維度、動機難測的奇異強大生物的稱謂。（「黛德拉」實際上是複數形式；單數為「黛德羅斯」。）在天際省國王「虔誠者哈勒」的一篇晚期論文中——距艾蕾西婭教義原版發布近千年——他將政敵的陰謀比作「奧伯利文惡魔的罪惡……其墮落堪比桑圭因本身，他們如博伊西婭般殘忍，如莫拉格‧巴爾般工於心計，如謝歐格拉思般瘋狂。」虔誠者哈勒如此冗長地將四位黛德拉領主引入了書面記載。

然而書面記載終究不是研究奧伯利文及其居民的最佳途徑。那些「與鬼魔來往」的人，鮮少願意讓此事成為公開記錄。儘管如此，在第一紀元的文獻中，散落著日記、日誌、巫術焚燒通告，以及殺黛德拉者的指南，這些是我主要的原始資料，它們至少與我親自召喚並長談過的黛德拉領主同樣可信。

奧伯利文顯然是由許多土地構成的地方——因此奧伯利文有許多同義稱謂：冷港、泥潭、月影，等等。每片奧伯利文的土地由一位王侯統轄，這一推論大概無誤。在古代記錄中反復出現的黛德拉王侯名字包括前述的桑圭因、博伊西婭、莫拉格‧巴爾、謝歐格拉思，以及阿祖拉、梅法拉、克拉維克斯‧維爾、瓦爾尼瑪、馬拉卡斯、赫爾麥斯（或赫爾麥烏斯或霍爾麥烏斯——似乎沒有一個公認的拼法）‧莫拉、納米拉、基加拉格、諾克特娜爾、梅魯恩斯‧達格恩，以及佩瑞特。

就我的經驗而言，黛德拉是非常複雜的存在，除了他們的龐大力量和偏激傾向外，幾乎無從整體歸類。梅魯恩斯‧達格恩、莫拉格‧巴爾、佩瑞特、博伊西婭與瓦爾尼瑪，在黛德拉之中屬於最一貫「惡魔性」的，因為他們的領域在本質上似乎是破壞性的。莫拉格‧巴爾選擇借助其他黛德拉的力量行事，博伊西婭則激勵凡人戰士的武力。

### 與 VIGILANT 的關聯

莫拉格‧巴爾的冷港（Coldharbour）在這部著作中首次被點名為奧伯利文的眾多土地之一。這篇文章也確立了「莫拉格‧巴爾最善於借助其他黛德拉行事」的評估——這正是 VIGILANT mod 中展現的行動模式：他不親自出手，而是透過吸血鬼、僕從與陷阱一一誘導玩家。值得注意的是，本書作者居然親自召喚過這些黛德拉領主並「長談」，這種學術式的狂妄正是 Sofia 最適合嘲諷的對象。

## Souls, Black and White / 《靈魂，黑與白》

**FormID**: 0x01AD0C　**EditorID**: Book4RareSoulsBlackAndWhite

### 英文原文

Souls, Black and White

The nature of the soul is not knowable. Every wizard that has attempted it vanishes without a trace. What can be known is that souls are a source of mystic energy that can be harvested.

Every creature, living or dead, is powered by a soul. Without it, they are just lumps of flesh or piles of bones. This animating force can be contained within a soul gem, if the soul gem has the capacity. From the gem, the power can be used to power magical items.

Centuries of experimentation has demonstrated that there are black souls and white souls. Only the rare black soul gem can hold the soul of a higher creature, such as a man or an elf. While the souls of lesser creatures can be captured by gems of many colors, they are all categorized as white soul gems. Hence the division of souls into black and white.

White souls are far safer than black souls, although not as powerful. Beginning students of Mysticism should not dabble in black souls or black soul gems. Even if one were to ignore the guild strictures against the necromatic arts used to power black soul gems, it is dangerous to the caster to handle them for long. If the gem is not precisely the size of the encased soul, small bits of the caster's soul may leak into the gem when it is touched.

### 繁體中文翻譯

《靈魂，黑與白》

靈魂的本質無從知曉。每一位嘗試探究此問題的術士都無影無蹤地消失了。可知的是：靈魂是一種神秘能量的來源，可以被採集。

每一個生靈，無論生死，皆由靈魂驅動。失去靈魂，它們不過是一堆肉塊或一堆骸骨。這種生命力可被封存在靈魂寶石中，只要寶石有足夠的容量。從寶石中，這股力量可被用來驅動魔法物品。

數個世紀的實驗證明，靈魂分為黑色與白色兩種。只有稀有的黑色靈魂寶石才能容納高等生靈的靈魂，如人類或精靈。低等生靈的靈魂雖可被各種顏色的寶石捕獲，但統稱為白色靈魂寶石。由此，靈魂被分為黑與白兩類。

白色靈魂遠比黑色靈魂安全，雖然效力不如後者強大。秘術初學者不應涉足黑色靈魂或黑色靈魂寶石。即便忽視公會對驅動黑色靈魂寶石之死靈術的禁令，長時間持握此類寶石對施術者本身也是危險的。若寶石尺寸與封存的靈魂不完全吻合，施術者在觸碰時，其靈魂的細微碎片可能會滲入寶石之中。

### 與 VIGILANT 的關聯

莫拉格‧巴爾的核心計畫——「收割凡人靈魂」——在神學上直接與靈魂寶石的運作原理相關。VIGILANT 中涉及靈魂囚禁的場景，其恐怖感正是建立在這篇文章確立的宇宙觀上：人類的靈魂（黑色靈魂）可以像物品一樣被捕獲、儲存、利用。最後一行關於「術士的靈魂碎片可能滲入寶石」的描述，更是為 VIGILANT 中與靈魂相關的劇情增添了切身的恐怖感。

## 各書籍之間的聯繫

### 吸血鬼起源脈絡

《拉美‧巴爾與無歇之死》是整個吸血鬼神話的源頭文本，確立了三個相互關聯的宇宙事件：莫拉格‧巴爾侵犯凡人——不死族誕生——阿卡依的失敗。《黛德拉之書》中對莫拉格‧巴爾的定義（「收割靈魂、傳播紛爭」）與《靈魂，黑與白》中對靈魂本質的分析形成閉環：巴爾不只創造了吸血鬼，他整個領域的本質就是對靈魂秩序的永久破壞。《論奧伯利文》則把他的冷港納入更大的奧伯利文地理框架，並補充了他「善於借助他人行事」的行事風格評估。

### 艾蕾西婭盟約脈絡

《聖艾蕾西婭的試煉》和《培林納之歌》諸卷共同構成了第一紀元末期反抗艾勒伊德統治的宏大史詩敘事。這個敘事的核心悖論是：艾蕾西婭的自由是以永久的神聖約束換來的（「你的血脈只要佩戴護符，龍焰便長燃」），而培林納的「自由」則以精神崩潰和最終的碎裂為代價。兩者都證明了一個貫穿 Elder Scrolls 宇宙觀的主題：凡人從神聖力量中獲得的任何恩賜，都帶有無法預見的代價。

### 黛德拉宇宙觀脈絡

《艾德拉與黛德拉》、《論奧伯利文》和《黛德拉之書》共同構建了理解黛德拉本質的知識框架。《艾德拉與黛德拉》提供最精簡的本體論（艾德拉=靜止+可殺；黛德拉=變化+只能驅逐），《論奧伯利文》在歷史維度上加以補充，《黛德拉之書》則提供了各王侯領域的名錄式定義。《靈魂，黑與白》則把這一切落實到最具體、最令人不安的層面：人的靈魂可以被作為資源使用。

### 梅麗迪亞的雙重性

梅麗迪亞在《培林納之歌》第三卷中扮演了意外的雙重角色：她的奧羅蘭天使與艾勒伊德人結盟，賦予烏瑪里爾不死之力；但她對不死族的根本厭惡（其領域與「生命體的能量」相關）又使她成為 VIGILANT 中反對莫拉格‧巴爾的自然盟友。理解梅麗迪亞的關鍵在於：她的善意是有條件的、是出於她自身的神學領域邏輯，而非對凡人的慈悲。

### VIGILANT 的聖典基礎

把這些書串聯起來，可以看見 VIGILANT mod 的故事建立在一個嚴密的神學邏輯上：莫拉格‧巴爾在時間之初便打破了生死秩序（拉美‧巴爾事件）——阿卡依的盟約提供了封印黛德拉的機制（艾蕾西婭盟約）——但黛德拉本質上只能被驅逐（《艾德拉與黛德拉》）——對抗這個問題需要同等神聖層級的力量（培林納的降臨、梅麗迪亞的介入）——而最終代價將永遠落在凡人身上。VIGILANT 並非憑空發明黑暗，而是忠實地延伸了 Bethesda 神學的邏輯。
