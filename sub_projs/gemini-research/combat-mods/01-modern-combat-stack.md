In 2026, the Skyrim combat modding landscape has moved beyond the experimental phase of early "Modern Combat" and settled into a highly optimized, modular "Action RPG" meta. The shift is characterized by moving away from script-heavy overhauls and towards engine-level behavior frameworks.

### 1. The "Old Guard": Role & Meta Status in 2026

*   **MCO/ADXP (Modern Combat Overhaul):** Now considered the **Legacy King**. While it pioneered "committed" attack animations, it is increasingly seen as the predecessor to **BFCO**. It remains the meta only for users who have massive, older animation libraries they don't want to convert.
*   **BFCO (Balanced Floating Combat Overhaul):** The **New Meta**. Released as a successor to MCO, it solves MCO's biggest flaws by natively supporting **jumping attacks, swimming attacks, and directional attacks** without buggy "patch" mods. It is faster, more stable, and allows NPCs to use custom animations without manual SCAR patching.
*   **TK Dodge RE:** Still the **standard** for dodging. The "RE" version is script-free (running via SKSE), making it the most responsive dodge mod in 2026.
*   **Valhalla Combat:** Still **essential**. It remains the gold standard for "Combat Logic," handling timed blocks, stamina management, and projectile parrying. It is now often used as a component of the "Plus Combat" stack.
*   **SCAR (Skyrim Combos AI Revolution):** Still **critical** for AI. Without SCAR 2.0, NPCs cannot perform the same combos as the player. Most modern BFCO setups have "SCAR-like" behavior built-in, but SCAR 2.0 (GitHub) is still used for complex custom AI packages.
*   **Precision:** **Non-negotiable**. It is the single most important combat mod, adding real physical hitboxes to weapons. In 2026, a modlist without Precision is considered "broken."
*   **Inpa Sekiro:** **Retired**. Due to its heavy script load and reliance on older frameworks (like Ultimate Combat), it has been replaced by more modular alternatives like **Sekiro Combat S** or the posture system in **Valhalla Combat**.

### 2. Newer Replacements & Innovations (2025–2026)

*   **Pandora Behavior Engine (Nexus: 133232):** The **Nemesis Killer**. It has completely replaced Nemesis and FNIS. It is significantly faster (compiles in seconds) and supports almost all legacy animations.
*   **OAR - Open Animation Replacer (Nexus: 92475):** The **DAR Successor**. It is the foundation for all modern animations, offering better performance and more complex animation conditions than the original DAR.
*   **Plus Combat (Nexus: 100915):** A modern "all-in-one" logic overhaul that seamlessly blends **Valhalla Combat** with **Chocolate Poise Plus** for a perfect balance of stamina and staggering.
*   **Sanguine Symphony (Nexus: 148388):** The 2026 standard for "Juice." It adds procedural blood, impact flashes, and directional hit sounds that make every strike feel heavy and impactful.
*   **Sky Tactics (Nexus: 131148):** Replaces older AI mods. It gives NPCs better positioning, making them flank the player and retreat when low on health.
